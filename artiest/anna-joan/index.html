Over

A multi-talented showstopper. Drawing inspiration from artists like FKA Twigs, COBRAH and Sevdaliza, Anna Joan is set to unleash her raw, dark electronic sound in '26. Her new, self-produced EP 'SKIN' is a sonic manifesto of sexual liberation. In her electronic performances she blends dance, live-music and outstanding vocal performances in a raw soundworld.
