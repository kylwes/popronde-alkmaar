Over

DE FORMULE will make nights last forever, by combining Electronic music with the raw energy of a band. Mixing songs like a DJ, but with the power of a Punk band. Inspired by Noisia and Limp Bizkit, DE FORMULE makes sure the crowd won’t stop moving.
